About the Author

This project was developed by Rachel Yanyi Zhang, a high school researcher from BASIS International School Shenzhen, with a deep interest in Indigenous history, especially Sámi history. Rachel was born in Finland, living there for ten years. Her connection has helped to inspire her curiosity about the Sámi people and the importance of making Sámi history more accessible. This map is designed to help visualize the historical northward contraction of Sámi settlement areas. The map was created using Python and the Folium library in Google Colab, and is hosted as a static website.
