Curation and Sources

The routes and markers on this map were curated from a synthesis of scholarly works on Sámi history and archaeology. Key references include:

  • Hansen, L. I., & Olsen, B. (2014). Hunters in Transition: An Outline of Early Sámi History.
  • Kent, N. (2014). The Sámi Peoples of the North: A Social and Cultural History.
  • Broadbent, N. (2010). Lapps and Labyrinths: Saami Prehistory, Colonization and Cultural Resilience.
  • Various archaeological reports on Comb Ceramic and Asbestos Ceramic cultures.

The periods indicated are approximate and reflect current academic consensus. Where no specific historical reason for movement is documented, the map indicates that movement was likely driven by seasonal resource availability, a fundamental aspect of Sámi semi‑nomadic lifeways.

All content has been reviewed for historical accuracy and cultural sensitivity.
