About this project

This interactive map visualizes the historical migration traces of the Sámi people, the Indigenous people of northern Fennoscandia. It is based on archaeological, linguistic, and historical research, showing plausible movement corridors from the southern Nordic region to the Arctic over millennia.

The map highlights ten distinct routes and includes detailed pop-ups with information about each location: the period of Sámi presence, the lifestyle adaptations to the local environment, reasons for movement, and interactions with contemporaneous Nordic populations.

The project aims to provide an accessible educational tool for understanding the long history and cultural resilience of the Sámi.
